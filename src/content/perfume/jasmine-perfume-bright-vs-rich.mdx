Start with the kind of jasmine you want to smell. If you're after a bright floral, choose a sample with citrus or another light floral around it. If you want a deeper white-flower perfume, try jasmine on a woody or amber base. Try a tea-style jasmine separately if the scent you have in mind is a cup of jasmine tea.

<h2 id="brightness">Bright jasmine and mixed white florals</h2>

In [a Reddit thread asking for jasmine perfumes](https://www.reddit.com/r/Perfumes/comments/1xzyuil/jasmine_scented_perfumes/), one reader found Gucci Bloom realistic, while another noticed more tuberose. A different commenter preferred Gucci Flora Gorgeous Jasmine for a brighter effect. [Gucci lists mandarin, two jasmine absolutes, and magnolia for Flora Gorgeous Jasmine](https://www.gucci.com/us/en/pr/beauty/beauty-makeup/gucci-flora-gorgeous-jasmine-100ml-eau-de-parfum-p-701224999990099). If jasmine is what you hope to smell most, try both on skin and see which flower leads for you. Gucci Bloom and Flora Gorgeous Jasmine are separate products.

<h2 id="richer-jasmine">Rich jasmine over woods</h2>

Mugler Alien Eau de Parfum came up repeatedly as a bold jasmine. [Mugler identifies jasmine sambac as the central floral note](https://www.mugler.com/fragrance/womens-fragrances/alien/alien-eau-de-parfum/M010401002.html), with woods and amber supporting it. A few readers reported strong performance on themselves. If that sounds appealing, try the original Eau de Parfum and start with a light application. Check how it smells at your usual distance and how close it sits later in the day.

<h2 id="tea">Jasmine tea and a side-by-side test</h2>

A separate [reader-made jasmine spectrum](https://www.reddit.com/r/Perfumes/comments/1xsulyy/i_made_a_jasmine_fragrance_spectrum/) prompted a suggestion for One Day Jasmine Tea. The commenter found the tea note strong enough to shape the whole fragrance. That is a good clue if you like leaves and brewed tea as much as the flower. The same thread also describes richer jasmines as indolic, a word readers use for the fleshy or animalic side of some white-flower scents.

Pick one brighter floral and either a woody jasmine or a tea-style scent. Test them one at a time and jot down what stands out after the opening.
