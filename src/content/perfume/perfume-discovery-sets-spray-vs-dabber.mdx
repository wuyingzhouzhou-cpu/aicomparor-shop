For someone who wants to try a different scent every day, pick a set of clearly labeled spray vials. For someone who collects miniature bottles, choose a dabber set if they enjoy applying perfume directly to skin. A set with a voucher can work when you know the recipient will be able to redeem it for a fragrance they like.

<h2 id="dabber-bottles">Dabber bottles for a keepsake gift</h2>

In [a separate discussion about TOCCA minis](https://www.reddit.com/r/Perfumes/comments/1y1e3xc/finally_got_my_hands_on_these_tocca_perfume_minis/), the person who bought Florence, Cleopatra, and Stella liked the little bottles. A decorative bottle can make the gift feel special, but give the applicator the same attention as the packaging. A dabber puts perfume on a small spot, while a spray lets the wearer mist the skin.

<h2 id="vouchers">Vouchers and a final buying check</h2>

If a voucher is included, read the eligible fragrance and size list, whether it can be redeemed online, and any deadline or location restrictions. A sampler works as a gift on its own when the recipient can enjoy the vials even if they never redeem anything.

Before checkout, check the format, scent list, and whether the recipient already owns one of the minis. Include shipping in your budget. If the retailer doesn't clearly say spray or dabber, look for a photo of the opened applicator or ask the seller.
