Lay out the brushes you used the last time you did your makeup. A head that you washed between two steps, or wished were clean for the next color, is a better clue to your next purchase than the number of brushes in a sale bundle.

<h2 id="inventory">Start with the two sets in the Reddit question</h2>

A shopper already owned Real Techniques Everyday Essentials and the EcoTools Eye Enhancing Duo. She wore eye makeup, concealer, blush and highlighter. She liked both sets and was considering buying them again. The overlap is easy to miss in a sale photo: the EcoTools duo already covers shading, blending, defining and smudging with four ends. A second copy makes sense if you switch colors often enough to want clean ends available. <h2 id="roles">Find the step where a clean head would help</h2>

**Blush followed by highlighter:** sweep the brush on a clean tissue after blush. If it still deposits pink where you want a pale highlight, a dedicated small cheek brush solves a real problem. **Concealer followed by powder shadow:** a creamy brush can make dry shadow go on patchily. A separate concealer head lets the shadow brush stay dry. **One neutral shadow most days:** a fresh copy of the entire eye set may spend the week in your drawer. <h2 id="replace">Replace a worn tool or buy a second set?</h2>

Check your most-used brush after washing and drying. A metal ferrule that rocks on the handle is a different problem from a second color clinging to sound bristles. In the Reddit exchange, commenters reported different experiences with wear; that cannot establish a defect rate. Replace a loose brush individually if its neighbors work well. If several heads are worn and every replacement would have a job, compare the full set price with those singles. For the most shape-sensitive purchase, see [how to choose a concealer head](/brushes/concealer-brush-shape).

If the gap is specifically eyeshadow and brows, the Real Techniques Eye Shade & Blend set is a smaller three-piece option. Check which of its heads would add a job you cannot already do with your EcoTools duo.
