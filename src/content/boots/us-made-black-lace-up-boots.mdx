Start with the **Silversmith 3361** if you want a black lace-up that shows above the ankle. Start with the **Iron Ranger 3366** if you like a cap toe and a shorter-looking boot under cuffed jeans. Both are in Red Wing’s women’s Heritage line. Look up the four-digit style number before comparing sellers: Iron Ranger 3365, for example, is brown.

<h2 id="request">What the shopper actually wanted</h2>

A shopper in r/madeinusa wanted one durable black boot for everyday outfits. Later in the thread she narrowed the brief to a taller shaft and a ceiling of about $400. When someone linked the Silversmith, she said its shape was what she had in mind. That last detail matters: a well-made short boot can still be the wrong purchase if most of it disappears under your hem. [Read the original exchange](https://www.reddit.com/r/madeinusa/comments/1xcz6xq/made_in_usa_looking_for_women_boots/).

<h2 id="models">What distinguishes the two pairs</h2>

The **Silversmith 3361** has a plain toe, black Boundary leather and laces that extend up the shaft. Red Wing lists a Goodyear leather welt and Vibram 430 Mini-Lug outsole. The **Iron Ranger 3366** puts a stitched leather cap over the toe. On black leather that seam is still visible under a trouser hem, even when the laces are hidden. Both product lines are identified as US-made by the manufacturer. [Silversmith specifications](https://www.redwingshoes.com/womens/heritage/silversmith-03361.html) · [Women’s Iron Ranger styles](https://www.redwingshoes.com/womens/heritage/iron-ranger/).

<h2 id="order">Three details to check before ordering</h2>

Match the listing to the women’s style number, then check the width offered in your size. If buying secondhand, ask for a photo of the interior label and a straight-on view of both heels. Uneven heel wear or an upcoming resole can erase the savings on an otherwise good pair. Finally, try the laces with the socks and jeans you actually wear. If you want to skip lacing entirely, compare [Chelsea and zip ankle boots](/boots/chelsea-boots-vs-ankle-boots).
